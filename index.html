    Frequently asked questions

    Is Biology Hub free?

    This prototype runs as a static webpage. Hosting it on a free static hosting service is possible. Future services may have their own limits.

    Why are some chapters marked coming soon?

    The chapter list is included, but each chapter needs its own checked question bank. A chapter should not be labelled quiz-ready until its questions are added.

    Will the leaderboard show everyone?

    Not yet. A shared leaderboard needs a secure online database and rules to prevent spam and fake scores.

    Can I use this for NEET preparation?

    Use the available quizzes for extra practice, and continue studying your NCERT textbook and trusted study material. This prototype does not yet cover the entire syllabus.
